Towns they tried to erase.

Thriving Black municipalities, districts, and settlements — built from nothing after Emancipation, many destroyed by violence, policy, or "progress." Each one gets its name sung back into history.

Allensworth

California

Allensworth — Colonel Allen Allensworth's 1908 Black city in the San Joaquin Valley; schoolhouse, stores, "we don't need permission to begin"; water cut, rail rerouted — sabotage by neglect; "a lesson in breath."

Texas

Barrett Station — freedmen's settlement under the stars; Barrett's name a beacon; railroad tracks and cane fields; gospel-fire churches; highways splitting the community; "the soil remembers all our names."
