How it's made

Research first. Each song starts with the real history of a Black town — its founding, its people, what it built, and what happened to it.

Human words. The lyrics are written by hand from that research, carrying names, dates, and details the history books left out.

New instruments. The music is composed with AI tools — a new instrument for an old mission: making sure these names are sung, remembered, and never erased.
